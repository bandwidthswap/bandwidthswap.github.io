What an operator sees

Every node that runs the kit serves its own dashboard at its own address, offline, with nothing loaded from anywhere else. There is one page and three views of it: the Mint's operator reads books, a node's operator reads a link, and someone running several nodes reads health and position across all of them. The pages below are snapshots of the lab's four nodes, taken on October 18, 2026, drawn by the same code a node serves; nothing on them is live.

  • The Mint's view

    bw-mint's books: what it sees now, what needs attention, the hour as it closed, seven days of traffic, each link's gap against its margin, the settled windows (a row opens to its receipt and its ack), the chain of committed hours with a proof you can verify in the page, the map with the Steward's offers, purchases, the Steward, the month's money and invoice.

    drag the "as of" slider to see the books as they stood at any hour

  • A hop's view

    bw-neighbor, the relay at the neighbor's house: my month and what I am owed, my link and each payer's standing against my patience, my windows as the Mint settled them, the purchases that govern my link, and the Mint from my side.

  • A payer's view

    bw-house, the node that buys: what I signed and what I owe, the hop I buy from and the terms my windows settled under, my purchases and their progress.

  • The fleet's view

    All four nodes from one operator's browser: the grid, attention across the fleet, the hour across every Mint, position (what my payers paid, what my hops earned, the net), and the map with my places marked. The list of nodes lives in the browser, never on a node.

To see your own nodes this way, run the kit: github.com/bandwidthswap/kit. A node's page is at http://<the node>:8710/, the fleet page at /fleet on any of your nodes, and /snapshot saves the page with its data as one file, which is what these are. The public views of the lab, the Lab status and the Steward's book, are fed by the Mint's public feed and carry names, roles, times and byte counts only.
